# LLMs for analog circuit design: downloadable literature
Search date: 2026-09-27. This is a best-effort research collection, not a guarantee of every publication.
**63 verified PDFs** are in `papers/`, including 8 supplied manually. The [CSV index](index.csv) records titles, authors, dates, source and PDF URLs, file sizes, SHA-256 checksums, and manual provenance. [Identified works without a local PDF](not_downloaded.md): 2.
Scope: LLM and language-model methods applied to analog or mixed-signal circuit topology, schematics, sizing, optimization, layout, testbenches, and supporting datasets. A small adjacent section covers general circuit-design assistants, analog in-memory design, and one gate-level digital circuit paper retained for comparison. Papers about building analog hardware to run LLMs were excluded.
Discovery used [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/), [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/), and the [Awesome-LLM-Circuit-Agent reading list](https://github.com/qhy991/Awesome-LLM-Circuit-Agent). Titles follow the current source version; some preprints have been retitled since their original release.
